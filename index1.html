Smart Parking System

MAHJOOZ

A smart parking system that connects a mobile application with a physical parking barrier, making it easier to find, reserve and access a parking space.

Explore the system
01 · The system

Application + Device

The app handles the parking reservation, while the physical device controls access to the reserved space.

02 · For who?

Two types of parking access

MAHJOOZ is designed to support accessible parking while also offering reservable paid parking for other users.

FREE · SPECIAL NEEDS

People with disabilities

Eligible users can access designated parking spaces for free. The system supports permanent disabilities and a verification process for temporary disabilities before access is approved.

PAID · VIP / GENERAL

Paid reserved parking

Other users can reserve an available parking space for a fee. This gives users a guaranteed space, especially when parking is busy or during events.

03 · How it works

From reservation to access

01

Find a space

The user opens the app and checks available parking locations.

02

Reserve

The user selects a space and confirms the reservation and payment when required.

03

Connect

The app connects to the smart parking device through Bluetooth.

04

Access

The user can raise or lower the parking barrier for the reserved space.
